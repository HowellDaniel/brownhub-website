Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you reach out.

What services does BrownHub offer?

Two studios under one roof: software — websites, web apps, cloud & DevOps, AI and data platforms — and graphic design & print: logos, branding, flyers, banners, pull-up stands, frames and book covers. See the services page and the catalog for details and samples.

How much does a new website cost?

It depends on scope — number of pages, features and deadline. Send us your idea by contact form, email or WhatsApp and you'll get a free consultation and a clear quote within 24 hours. Design jobs are usually quoted the same day.

How long does a project take?

Typical turnaround: flyers and banners 1–3 days, logos and branding about a week, websites 1–3 weeks depending on page count. Need it faster? Ask about rush jobs when you request a quote.

How do I order a print or design item?

Pick the item in our catalog, tap “View details”, then press “Order now via chat” — or send your sizes, quantity and deadline through the contact form. We confirm the price and start right away.

Do you support products after launch?

Yes. Every build includes a handover period, and we offer ongoing maintenance, updates and hosting support so your website or platform keeps running smoothly after launch.
