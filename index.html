Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you reach out.

What design services does BrownHub offer?

Logos and full brand identity, social media graphics, flyers and posters, business cards, brochures and company profiles, packaging, marketing and advertising graphics, book covers and inside layout, plus large format print: pull-up banners, ABS boards and frames. We also build websites and software for brands that need them. See the services page and the catalog.

What does a design project cost?

It depends on the job — the number of items, sizes, quantity and deadline. Send us your idea by contact form, email or WhatsApp and you'll get a free consultation and a clear quote; design jobs are usually quoted the same day.

How long does a project take?

Typical turnaround: flyers and posters 1–3 days, logos and branding about a week, books and company profiles depend on page count. Need it faster? Ask about rush jobs when you request a quote.

How do I order a print or design item?

Pick the item in our catalog, tap “View details”, then press “Order now via chat” — or send your sizes, quantity and deadline through the contact form. We confirm the price and start right away.

Do you provide the files for printing?

Yes. You receive print-ready files in the right color mode and resolution, plus the source files, so you can reprint or edit whenever you need to — with us or with any printer.
