Technology people, building for people

BrownHub is a software company founded on a simple belief: great technology should be accessible to every ambitious business, not just the giants.

Our story

Founded in 2014 by a small team of engineers frustrated with bloated agencies and half-finished projects, BrownHub set out to do things differently: small senior teams, honest timelines, and code we'd be proud to maintain.

Twelve years later, we've shipped over 120 products for startups, scale-ups, and enterprises — and we still answer every client message ourselves.

Our mission

To help businesses win with software. That means building products that are fast, secure, and delightful — and leaving every client's team stronger than we found it.

We measure success not by lines of code, but by the outcomes our clients achieve: revenue grown, hours saved, customers delighted.
