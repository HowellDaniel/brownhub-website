    Frequently asked questions

    Quick answers before you reach out.

    Do you design the brand and build the website?

    Yes. Identity, print and screens come out of the same studio, so the website matches the logo instead of being reinterpreted by someone else.

    What does a website cost in Ghana?

    It depends on the number of pages, the features and whether the content already exists. Send us the brief and you get a free consultation and a clear quote before anything starts.

    Can you improve a website I already have?

    Yes. Send the address and what bothers you about it — we can refresh the design, fix the mobile layout, make it load faster, or rebuild only the parts that hold it back.

    Do you keep the site running after launch?

    Hosting, updates, backups and small changes are what the maintenance support covers. You keep the domain, the logins and the files either way.

    Still deciding?

    Send the idea as it is in your head — you get a free consultation and an honest yes or no within a day.
