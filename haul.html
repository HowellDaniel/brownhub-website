BrownHub Studio · Luggage service

Your luggage travels ahead of you.

BrownHub Haul collects your bags from your home or office in Accra, moves them on a live manifest, and hands them over at the airport, a hotel or the next city — against a four-digit PIN. It is the studio's own app: the same people who design the brand also run the bags.

How a haul works

Four steps, one manifest, no queue at the airport.

1

Book a pickup

A collector is rostered the night before and calls with a two-hour window.

2

We seal and tag

Each piece is weighed and sealed at your door. The tag matches the code on your receipt.

3

It moves

Your bags ride a locked manifest leg. The tracker changes stage as they move.

4

PIN handover

The driver releases the bags only against your four-digit PIN.

Where it goes

Built around how people actually leave Accra.

Kotoka International

Bags reach the BrownHub desk on Level 1 and wait for you — no check-in queue with a suitcase.

Your hotel

Delivered to the front desk against the PIN, so a late flight does not mean a late bag.

Another city

Kumasi, Takoradi, Tamale and the cross-border leg — priced on distance, not guesswork.

What it costs

A quote before you commit: base handling, distance, weight, pieces, and 15% VAT on top. Transit cover is optional and priced on the declared value.
