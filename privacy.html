    Updated 27 September 2026. Plain summary of what this site stores and who it hands information to.

    What you send us

    The contact form, the chat assistant and the Project details voice notes carry whatever you type or record: your name, email, phone number, company, the service you want and the brief itself. We use it to answer you and to price and deliver the work. The assistant also keeps each chat question and its answer, with no name attached, so the studio can see what people ask about.

    Client accounts

    The "My requests" panel is optional. If you sign up, your email address and a protected password are stored with our account provider, Supabase, together with the requests you send so you can read them again and re-order. You may add a phone number and confirm it by text message; that number is used only to contact you about your own requests.

    Payments

    Card and mobile money payments are taken by Paystack inside their own secure checkout. BrownHub receives the item, the amount and a payment reference. Card numbers, PINs and bank details never pass through this website and we never store them.

    Where information goes

    Form submissions are relayed to our email by FormSubmit. Accounts, request history and anonymous chat transcripts live with Supabase. Payments are processed by Paystack. Text messages are delivered through the SMS provider configured by Supabase. Each of these companies has its own privacy policy and keeps data under its own terms.

    Storage on your device

    This site saves a few small things in your browser: the language you picked, the dark or light theme, your cookie choice, a sign-in session while you are logged in, and a random code that keeps the chat assistant's answers to you in one thread. None of those is an advertising cookie, and you can clear all of them in your browser at any time.

    Deleting your information

    Email us from the address on your account and we will delete your account and the requests attached to it. Enquiries sent through the contact form can be removed from our mailbox on request.
